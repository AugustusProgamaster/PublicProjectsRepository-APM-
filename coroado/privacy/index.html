Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Effective 24 November 2026

In short. Coroado is a free game with no ads, no purchases, no sign-up and no game server of its own. The game does not send personal data to us or to anyone else. Your progress is saved only on your device.

We only process personal data when you write to support or take part in a closed test (beta), and only for those purposes.

1. Who is responsible

Coroado is created and published by Lucas Augusto, an independent developer who goes by AugustusProgamaster, a natural person resident in Portugal (“we”). We are the controller (GDPR, Art. 4(7); LGPD, Art. 5, VI) of the data described in this policy.

Privacy contact: augustusprogamaster@gmail.com. This address is also the communication channel with data subjects required by the Brazilian LGPD (Art. 41). We have not appointed a data protection officer because our processing does not fall under the mandatory cases (GDPR, Art. 37; ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 2/2022, Art. 11).

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Coroado game on every platform where it is distributed (for example Android and web browsers), to e-mail support and to the game’s closed tests. It does not apply to third-party websites, stores or social networks you reach through links, which follow their own policies.

3. What the game does with data

  • Progress and preferences stay on your device. Gems, unlocked items, achievements, defeated bosses, language and settings are written to the app’s local storage on your device. They are not sent to us, we have no access to them and they do not identify you.
  • No account, no login. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or date of birth and does not create a player profile.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The game contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs, does not use the device advertising ID (the AD_ID permission is removed on Android) and uses no cookies. Since there is no tracking, “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control signals change nothing: the game already works as if you had refused all tracking.
  • No game server. Matches, including computer-controlled opponents, run entirely on your device. The game works offline.
  • No sensitive permissions. The game does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. Vibration is used only as a game effect.
  • External links. The “About” and privacy screens contain links (GitHub, LinkedIn, this policy, support e-mail). They open only when you tap them, in your device’s browser or e-mail app, outside the game.

5. App stores and operating system

The operator of the app store where you get Coroado (for example, the Android app store) processes your data as an independent controller when you download, update or review the game, under its own privacy policy. The same applies to your device’s operating system, which may back up app data (including your Coroado progress) to the account set up on the device if that feature is on. We do not control this processing and have no access to those backups.

6. Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share personal data for advertising, including cross-context behavioural advertising. The only third parties that may come into contact with the data in section 4 are our e-mail provider and the store operator that manages the tester list, acting as our processors or as independent controllers, as the case may be. We may also disclose data when required by law, a court order or a competent authority, or to defend legal claims.

7. International transfers

Our e-mail provider and the store operator may keep data on servers outside the European Economic Area and Brazil. These transfers rely on an adequacy decision (for example the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, GDPR Art. 45) or on standard contractual clauses (GDPR, Art. 46(2)(c)) and, under the LGPD, on standard contractual clauses and the other grounds in Art. 33 and ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 19/2024. You may ask about the applicable safeguards at the contact address.

8. Retention

  • Data on your device: until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the game.
  • Support messages: up to 2 years after the last contact, unless we need them longer to establish or defend legal claims.
  • Tester data: for the duration of the test and up to 12 months after it ends, or for the term of the confidentiality agreement, whichever is longer. Feedback about the game may be kept in anonymised form without limit.

9. Children and teenagers

Coroado is intended for people aged 13 and over and is designed to be safe at any age: it collects no personal data, shows no advertising, has no chat or interaction between players, no purchases, no loot boxes or paid random mechanics, no behavioural profiling and no compulsive engagement techniques tied to money.

We do not knowingly accept support messages from, or test participation by, people below the minimum age to consent on their own to data processing in their country. That age varies: 13 in the US (COPPA), Portugal (Law 58/2019, Art. 16) and the UK; between 14 and 16 in other EU countries (GDPR, Art. 8); 18 in India (DPDP Act); and in Brazil, processing data of children (up to 12) requires specific consent from a parent or guardian (LGPD, Art. 14). If you are under the age for your country, please ask a parent or guardian to write on your behalf.

If we learn that we received a child’s data without the required authorisation, we will delete it. Parents and guardians may ask for access or deletion at any time through the contact in section 1.

This section also addresses, to the extent they apply to a game that collects no data: the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children’s Code), EU rules for services that minors may access (including Article 28 of the Digital Services Act, where applicable), and Brazil’s Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (Law 15,211/2025): no targeted advertising, no profiling, no loot boxes and the most protective settings by default.

10. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask us, free of charge, for:

  • confirmation that we process your data, and access to it (including a copy);
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • deletion, anonymisation, blocking or restriction of processing;
  • portability of data you provided to us, in a structured format;
  • objection to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • information about whom we share data with and about the option not to consent;
  • withdrawal of consent, where processing relies on it;
  • not to be subject to solely automated decisions (we make none);
  • nominating someone to exercise your rights after your death, where the law provides for it (for example in India and France).

To exercise any right, write to augustusprogamaster@gmail.com. We reply within the shortest legal deadline that applies to you and in any case within 15 days where the LGPD applies (Art. 19, II), one month under the GDPR (Art. 12(3)) and 45 days under US state laws, which may be extended as each law allows. We may ask for minimal information to confirm your identity. We do not discriminate against anyone who exercises their rights. If we deny a request, we explain why and how to appeal.

You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country. Section 11 lists the main ones.

12. Security

We collect as little as possible and keep no player database. Access to the contact mailbox is restricted to the controller and protected by two-step verification. Data on your device is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by the operating system.

13. Changes to this policy

If Coroado gains features that process data, such as online multiplayer, accounts or crash reports, this policy will be updated before those features go live and the change will be announced in the game. The effective date at the top always shows the current version.
